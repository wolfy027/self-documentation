# Polish A1 - revision cheat sheet

## Sounds & stress
*Polish is spelled exactly as it sounds*

> Good news: Polish is phonetic. Learn about 30 sounds once and you can read any word aloud. No silent letters.
- STRESS = the second-to-last syllable
- PRO-szę | please
- dzię-KU-ję | thank you
- u-ni-WER-sy-tet | university
- LETTER | SOUNDS LIKE | EXAMPLE
- ą | nasal “on” | są = they are
- ę | nasal “en” (at the end: “eh”) | proszę = please
- ó | exactly like u: “oo” | córka = daughter
- y | short i, as in bit | dobry = good
- LETTER | SOUNDS LIKE | EXAMPLE
- ł | English “w” | mały = small  (MAH-wih)
- w | English “v” | woda = water  (VOH-dah)
- j | English “y” | jak = how  (yahk)
- c | “ts” as in cats | co = what  (tsoh)
- ch / h | rough “h” in the throat | chleb = bread  (hlep)
- SPELLING | SOUNDS LIKE | EXAMPLE
- sz | “sh” | szkoła = school  (SHKOH-wah)
- cz | “ch” | czas = time  (chahs)
- ż  /  rz | “zh” as in pleasure | żona = wife  (ZHOH-nah)
- dż | English “j” | dżem = jam  (jem)
- szcz | “shch” – say both | szczęście = luck
- SOFT SOUND | SOUNDS LIKE | EXAMPLE
- ś / si | soft “sh” | siostra = sister  (SHOH-strah)
- ć / ci | soft “ch” | ciocia = aunt  (CHOH-chah)
- ź / zi | soft “zh” | zima = winter  (ZHEE-mah)
- ń / ni | “ny” as in canyon | nie = no  (nyeh)
- dź / dzi | soft “j” | dziecko = child  (JETS-koh)

## To be: być
*The most important verb in the language*

- ja | ty | on / ona / ono | my | wy | oni / one
- jestem - `YES-tem` - I am
- jesteś - `YES-tesh` - you are
- jest - `yest` - he / she / it is
- jesteśmy - `yes-TESH-mih` - we are
- jesteście - `yes-TESH-cheh` - you (pl) are
- są - `sohm` - they are
- Nie ma “a” or “the”! | Jestem studentem. = I am a student.
- Pronouns are optional | Jestem Tom. = I (am) Tom.
- Adjectives match gender | Jestem zmęczony / zmęczona
- Negative: put nie first | Nie jestem głodny. = I am not hungry.

## Gender & plurals
*Three genders, three colours*

- GENDER | USUAL ENDING  →  EXAMPLES
- MASCULINE  (m) | consonant: dom · rower · pies · stół
- FEMININE  (f) | -a: kawa · książka · woda · siostra
- NEUTER  (n) | -o / -e: okno · miasto · piwo · morze
> Exceptions: mężczyzna (man) looks feminine but is masculine; noc (night) looks masculine but is feminine.
- SINGULAR  →  PLURAL | RULE
- dom → domy | m: add -y  (house → houses)
- rower → rowery | m: add -y  (bike → bikes)
- kawa → kawy | f: -a becomes -y
- książka → książki | f: -i after k and g
- okno → okna | n: -o becomes -a
- miasto → miasta | n: -o becomes -a
> Learn these by heart: dziecko → dzieci (child → children), człowiek → ludzie (person → people), brat → bracia (brother → brothers).

## Café scene
*Everything together: listen, then say the customer lines*

- Dzień dobry! Co podać? | jen DOH-bri! tsoh POH-dahch | Good day! What can I get you?
- Poproszę kawę z mlekiem. | poh-PROH-sheh KAH-veh z MLEH-kyem | A coffee with milk, please.
- Coś jeszcze? | tsosh YESH-cheh | Anything else?
- Tak, szklankę wody. | tahk SHKLAHN-keh VOH-dih | Yes, a glass of water.
- To będzie dwadzieścia złotych. | toh BEN-jeh dvah-JESH-chah ZWOH-tihkh | That will be twenty zloty.
- Proszę. Dziękuję! | PROH-sheh. jen-KOO-yeh | Here you go. Thanks!
- Smacznego! Miłego dnia! | smahch-NEH-goh! mee-WEH-goh dnyah | Enjoy! Have a nice day!
> Spot the cases: kawę accusative · z mlekiem instrumental · wody genitive · złotych genitive plural.
